Welcome to **BioPath Analytica**, where science meets clarity and support.  

I founded BioPath Analytica to help biologists—especially junior researchers—navigate the challenges of data analysis and research life. Our mission is to combine rigorous **bioinformatics expertise** with a touch of **holistic guidance**, so you can not only solve technical problems but also thrive in your scientific journey.  

---

### 🌱 Our App in Development
I’m currently developing a tool designed to guide biologists in matching their **experimental data** with the **most suitable analysis methods** for their research goals. This app aims to simplify decision-making, reduce trial-and-error, and give junior scientists a clearer roadmap in bioinformatics.  

But science is not just about code and pipelines. It’s also about resilience, perspective, and creativity. That’s why a second part of the app is dedicated to **daily guidance for research professionals**.  

---

### 🔮 A Daily Guide for Positive Spirits
This part of the app draws inspiration from **Zhou Yi (I Ching)** philosophy—an ancient framework not in conflict with science, but a way of observing patterns in nature and aligning our actions with them.  

Using the **六爻 (Six Lines)** prediction method, the app provides **holistic daily suggestions** for biologists. These insights are not technical solutions, but gentle reminders to approach experiments and projects with fresh thinking and positivity.  

For example:  
> *You receive “水雷屯” followed by “泽地萃.” Today may feel like a slow start or even stuckness in preparing materials or writing. But the second gua suggests support from experienced colleagues—asking for help could unlock insights that move your work forward.*  

These daily guides encourage **positive thinking, creative problem-solving, and resilience** in the face of setbacks—qualities every scientist needs.
